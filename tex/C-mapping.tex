\section{Mapping from the Projective Gap $\Delta_\Pi$ to the ARPES Gap
  $\Delta(\mathbf{k})$}
  \label{app:mapping}

  \subsection{Definition Recalled}
    \label{subsec:definition-recalled}

    The projective gap $\Delta_\Pi \equiv \lambda_{\min}(L_\Pi)|_{w=2}$ is the lowest positive
    eigenvalue of the linearised relaxation operator restricted to the $w = 2$ fiber sector
    (Eq.~(7) of the main text).
    $L_\Pi$ acts on fiber-phase fluctuations $\delta\theta(\mathbf{r})$ around the coherent
    composite background; its spectrum encodes the local cost of fiber deformation.

  \subsection{Isotropic Limit: Recovery of BCS}
    \label{subsec:isotropic-limit:-recovery-of-bcs}

    In the absence of anisotropic frustration ($r_F = 0$), the relaxation operator $L_\Pi$ is
    spatially uniform, its eigenfunctions are plane waves $e^{i\mathbf{k}\cdot\mathbf{r}}$, and
    the eigenvalue is $\mathbf{k}$-independent at leading order:
    \begin{equation}
      \lambda(\mathbf{k})\big|_{r_F=0} \;=\; \Delta_\Pi^{(0)} \;=\; \text{const.}
      \label{eq:DeltaPi_iso}
    \end{equation}
    This corresponds to a nodeless, fully isotropic quasiparticle gap, recovering the BCS
    identification $\Delta_\textrm{BCS} = \Delta_\Pi^{(0)}$ of Section~3.2.

  \subsection{Anisotropic Regime and Emergence of Nodal Structure}
    \label{subsec:anisotropic-regime-and-emergence-of-nodal-structure}

    When staggered frustration is present ($r_F > 0$), the operator acquires a
    $\mathbf{k}$-dependent correction:
    \begin{equation}
      L_\Pi(\mathbf{k}) \;=\; L_\Pi^{(0)} + r_F\, V(\mathbf{k};\mathbf{Q}),
      \label{eq:Lpi_k}
    \end{equation}
    where $V(\mathbf{k};\mathbf{Q})$ is the fiber-phase mismatch kernel evaluated at wavevector
    $\mathbf{Q}$.
    First-order perturbation theory gives
    \begin{equation}
      \lambda(\mathbf{k}) \;=\; \Delta_\Pi^{(0)}
      + r_F\,\langle \mathbf{k}\,|\,V(\cdot;\mathbf{Q})\,|\,\mathbf{k}\rangle
      + \mathcal{O}(r_F^2).
      \label{eq:lambda_k}
    \end{equation}
    For $\mathbf{Q} = (\pi,\pi)$ and $B_{1g}$ symmetry, the matrix element evaluates to
    $\langle \mathbf{k}|V|\mathbf{k}\rangle \propto (\cos k_x - \cos k_y)$, so that
    \begin{equation}
      \Delta_\Pi(\mathbf{k}) \;\equiv\; \lambda(\mathbf{k})
      \;\propto\; \Delta_\Pi^{(0)}\,|\cos k_x - \cos k_y|.
      \label{eq:DeltaPi_d}
    \end{equation}

  \subsection{Identification with the Measured ARPES Gap}
    \label{subsec:identification-with-the-measured-arpes-gap}

    The quasiparticle gap measured by ARPES at Fermi momentum $\mathbf{k}_F$ equals the energy
    cost of creating a single $w = 1$ excitation from the composite, which equals
    $\lambda(\mathbf{k}_F)$.
    The identification is therefore
    \begin{equation}
      \Delta(\mathbf{k}) \;=\; \Delta_\Pi(\mathbf{k}) \;=\; \lambda(\mathbf{k})\big|_{w=2}.
      \label{eq:mapping}
    \end{equation}
    Three consequences follow directly from Eq.~\eqref{eq:mapping}.

    \textbf{(1) Isotropic BCS case.}
    $\Delta(\mathbf{k}) = \Delta_\Pi^{(0)}$: nodeless and fully isotropic, consistent with
    Eq.~\eqref{eq:DeltaPi_iso}.

    \textbf{(2) Nodal $d$-wave (cuprates).}
    $\Delta(\mathbf{k}) \propto |\cos k_x - \cos k_y|$ vanishes along the zone diagonal
    $(k_x = \pm k_y)$, reproducing the four nodal points observed in cuprate ARPES, with the
    maximal gap at $(\pi,0)$ and $(0,\pi)$.

    \textbf{(3) Extended $s^\pm$ (nickelates).}
    For $r_F < r_F^*$ the selected irrep is $A_{1g}^\pm$, giving
    $\Delta(\mathbf{k}) \propto |\cos k_x + \cos k_y|$: the gap changes sign between electron
    and hole pockets but is nodeless on each individual Fermi sheet, consistent with the
    intermediate disorder sensitivity predicted in Section~7.4.

    The proportionality constant between $\Delta_\Pi^{(0)}$ and the observed maximum gap
    amplitude is set by the raccordement cost scale.
    From Section~4.1, $\Delta_\Pi^\textrm{amplitude} \sim J$, consistent with the empirical ratio
    $\Delta_{\max}/k_BT_c \sim 4$--$6$ in cuprates when combined with the BKT relation
    $k_BT_c \simeq \frac{\pi}{2}\rho_s$ and the leading-order stiffness $\rho_s \sim \delta J$.
